# FinSignal Chrome Extension — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-06_

**The extension collects no user data.**

- No analytics, no tracking, no cookies set by the extension.
- Nothing is transmitted to the extension's developer.
- `storage` is used only locally in your browser: the app URL you configure
  (synced via your Chrome profile) and the most recently right-clicked
  ticker (session-only, cleared when the browser closes).
- The right-click menu receives the text you selected only at the moment
  you invoke it, to detect a ticker symbol. It is not stored beyond the
  session value above and never leaves your browser except as part of the
  app URL below.

The side panel embeds the FinSignal web application (by default the public
demo, or a deployment you configure). Questions you type there are
processed by that web application under its own terms; the extension adds
no data of its own. The public demo processes queries against public SEC
filings and applies daily usage limits; it does not require an account.
